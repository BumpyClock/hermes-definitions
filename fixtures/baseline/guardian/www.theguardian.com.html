A short synthetic river survey summary.

The river survey records clear water at three stations and notes the height of each marker. This original text tests substantive article preservation without retaining a publisher's prose.

Volunteers return to the same bridge each week, photograph the marker, and compare the readings. The record includes café notes and Unicode text for deterministic extraction.
